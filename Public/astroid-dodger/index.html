🎮 How to Play

📱 Mobile / Tablet

  • Move: Drag your finger up/down on the screen
  • Shoot: Tap the screen quickly
  • Goal: Collect 10 coins to advance!

💻 PC / Keyboard

  • Move: W/S or Arrow Up/Down
  • Shoot: Spacebar
  • Pause: P
  • Start: Enter or Spacebar

💡 Tips

  • Shoot asteroids to get bonus coins!
  • Watch out for faster asteroids in later levels
  • Planet Zeeb is waiting for you - collect coins to get closer!
